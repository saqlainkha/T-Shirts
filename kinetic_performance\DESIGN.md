## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-velocity technical athleticism. It bridges the raw, explosive energy of high-intensity training with the precision engineering of elite running gear. The target audience comprises dedicated athletes, runners, and performance enthusiasts who value technical craftsmanship and minimalist power.

The aesthetic combines **High-Contrast Bold Minimalism** with technical brutalist cues:
- Monolithic typography dominates the visual plane, conveying raw speed and unyielding momentum.
- Layouts balance razor-sharp contrast between pure deep carbon and clinical stark white, punctuated by electric, hyper-charged energetic accents.
- Surfaces are architectural and disciplined—eschewing unnecessary skeuomorphism, soft shadows, or playful curves in favor of structural clarity, high-density performance specifications, and impactful editorial imagery.

## Colors
The color architecture drives immediate focus and technical urgency through stark, high-contrast values:

- **Carbon Canvas (`#0A0A0A`, `#111111`)**: Deep, low-reflectance structural background layers that evoke athletic track surfaces and carbon-fiber plates.
- **Electric Volt (`#D4FF00`)**: Primary conversion trigger. Applied to high-priority interactive components, buy actions, active metrics, and critical telemetry feedback.
- **Hyper Orange (`#FF4D00`)**: Secondary energetic accelerator. Reserved for state urgency, drop announcements, heat zones, and high-impact visual callouts.
- **Athletic Neutral Scale**:
  - `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`: Maximum contrast display headers and primary editorial copy.
  - `Cold Gray High (`#F4F4F5`)`: Secondary surface contrast and stark inverted cards.
  - `Zinc Border (`#27272A`)`: Crisp structural dividers, architectural framing, and technical grids.
  - `Muted Technical (`#71717A`)`: Specifications, secondary metadata, and unit tags.

## Typography
Typographic execution leverages a deliberate dual-engine structure:

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Anton`)**: Always styled in strict uppercase with tight letter-spacing. Headings evoke stadium signage, digital split-flap timers, and technical racing telemetry. They demand rapid comprehension and exude physical authority.
- **Body & Specification Data (`Inter`)**: Engineered for ultra-clean readability against deep black fields. Numerical telemetry, sizing specifications, and materials descriptions rely on tabular numerical alignments and disciplined vertical rhythm.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: High-tracking uppercase labels establish visual rhythm for categories, filters, and performance badges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid built on strict architectural alignments:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 1.5rem gutters and generous 3rem outer margins to frame full-bleed imagery and product silhouettes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 1rem gutters and 2rem outer canvas margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns with 0.75rem gutters and 1.25rem canvas margins.

Spacing follows an unyielding geometric discipline. Product imagery and hero panels utilize intentional negative space to simulate the open runway of high-performance tracks, while data clusters and technical specs lock into dense, unified blocks.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through high-contrast structural framing, tonal layering, and tactical borders rather than diffuse ambient shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Technical Outlines**: Interactive containers, spec tables, and cards use crisp 1px borders (`#27272A` or `#1F1F23`) to define physical bounds without breaking the flat, aerodynamic profile.
- **High-Contrast Surface Stacking**: Overlays and sticky navigation bars use solid carbon tones (`#111111`) or 85% frosted backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over content streams.
- **Z-Index Precision**: Dynamic sticky cart bars, navigation, and floating telemetry pills maintain absolute elevation purely through z-index segregation and stark border contrasts.

## Shapes
Sharpness conveys speed, discipline, and uncompromising engineering.

- **Primary Geometry (Sharp / 0px)**: Cards, image containers, input fields, structural dividers, and primary call-to-action buttons feature zero-radius corners. This maintains tension and angular energy across all screens.
- **Exception Rule (Pill Badges)**: Status pills (e.g., "LIMITED DROP", "WATERPROOF", "CARBON PLATE") and filter tags use full capsule radii (`rounded-full`) to contrast deliberately against the rigid squared grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric Volt)**: Sharp rectangular profile, background `#D4FF00`, text `#0A0A0A`, typography `label-lg` uppercase. Hover shifts to `#CCFF00` with subtle rightward chevron micro-translate.
- **Secondary Action (Carbon Outline)**: Sharp profile, 1px border `#FFFFFF`, transparent background, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover inverts to background `#FFFFFF` and text `#0A0A0A`.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Monospaced-style Inter label with underlined kinetic accent on hover.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Tiles**: Sharp zero-radius containers featuring high-exposure neutral gray or black photography backdrops (`#141414`).
- **Telemetry Badges**: Pinned to top-left corner using high-contrast pill geometry (`#0A0A0A` border with `#FFFFFF` text or solid `#FF4D00` for urgency).
- **Price & Title Lockup**: Anton headline for product model names coupled with tabular Inter numbers for pricing.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields**: Sharp 1px bottom or framed border (`#27272A`), dark surface (`#111111`), placeholder in `#71717A`. Active/focus transitions border directly to `#D4FF00` with no ambient blur.
- **Size Selector Grid**: Sharp modular square tiles. Selected states flip instantly to solid white fill with inverted black text.

### Navigation & Cart Drawer
- **Navbar**: High-profile sticky header with monolithic brand wordmark, streamlined navigation links in uppercase `label-md`, and fixed icon badges with numeric notification dots in `#FF4D00`.
- **Telemetry Panels**: Structured tabular grids with monospaced data readouts showing weight, breathability indices, and carbon-plate stiffness ratings.
